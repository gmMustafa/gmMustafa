### 🛠️ Featured projects

**🏭 [FabPilot](https://github.com/gmMustafa/fabpilot): AI maintenance copilot for factories**<br>
A LangGraph agent reads sensor data (TimescaleDB) and manuals and past incidents (hybrid RAG, pgvector) through two MCP servers, finds the root cause with citations, and drafts a work order for human approval.<br>
On 51 fault scenarios in a CI eval suite: **86% root-cause accuracy, 0 false alarms on healthy machines, 10/10 misleading-question traps passed, 100% valid citations**. Three free models were compared with McNemar tests to pick the default.<br>
`LangGraph` `MCP` `pgvector` `TimescaleDB` `FastAPI` `Next.js` `Docker` `Helm`

**🐦 [Few-Shot Bird Species Classification](https://github.com/gmMustafa/Few-Shot-Learning-Paradigms-for-Bird-Species-Classification-under-Data-Scarcity): MSc thesis** · [▶ live dashboard](https://fsl-birds.streamlit.app)<br>
Can a model recognise a bird it has seen only once? A controlled comparison of Prototypical, Matching and Relation Networks against supervised and nearest-centroid baselines on Birds-20 and CUB-200, with ResNet-50 and DeiT-S.<br>
Prototypical Networks reach **94.1% at 1-shot and 99.8% at 5-shot**, on par with the fully supervised model. Runs on FAU's HPC GPU cluster.<br>
`PyTorch` `ResNet-50` `DeiT-S` `Meta-learning` `SLURM` `Streamlit`

**🎤 [PitchBuddy](https://github.com/gmMustafa/PitchBuddy): pitch your startup to a skeptical AI VC**<br>
Every reply has two voices: a VC who goes for your weakest assumption, and a coach who tells you how to answer better. 5 investor personas × 4 founder stages, Google Sign-In or guest mode, saved history.<br>
Built to run free in public: model fallback, token caps and rate-limit retries. **2nd of 16 teams at GDG Nuremberg Hackathon.**<br>
`LangChain` `Groq` `Streamlit` `Firestore` `Google OAuth`

**📄 [HireReady](https://hire-ready-fe.vercel.app): AI resume analyzer**<br>
Scores resumes for ATS fit (0–100) with a 5-stage NLP pipeline, then Llama 3.3 70B writes suggestions and rewrites. Runs at zero hosting cost.<br>
`KeyBERT` `spaCy` `sentence-transformers` `Groq` `React` `FastAPI` `Supabase`
